Training Programs

UMS Training

Serving on the AUVSI subcommittee responsible for developing the curriculum and safety standards for uncrewed maritime operations. Safe Harbor Maritime is the first West Coast provider of AUVSI-accredited UMS Trusted Operator Program. Leveraging our extensive real-world experience operating and deploying UMS platforms, our training delivers practical, hands-on instruction aligned with industry-recognized standards.

Foundational

Routine UMS Operations

Training for day-to-day uncrewed maritime system operations, safety procedures, and mission execution.

Key Topics

  • System familiarization and operation
  • Safety protocols and procedures
  • Mission planning fundamentals
  • Emergency response training
Advanced

Advanced & BVLOS USV Operations

Advanced training focused on long-range, autonomous, and Beyond Visual Line of Sight surface vessel operations.

Key Topics

  • BVLOS operational protocols
  • Long-range mission planning
  • Autonomous system management
  • Complex environmental operations
Advanced

Advanced & BVLOS UUV Operations

Advanced subsurface training covering autonomous missions, extended endurance operations, and complex underwater environments.

Key Topics

  • Underwater navigation systems
  • Extended endurance missions
  • Subsea sensor operations
  • Deep water operational protocols
